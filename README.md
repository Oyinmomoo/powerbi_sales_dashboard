# Sales & Profitability Analysis

## Project Overview
An end-to-end sales analytics project leveraging Microsoft Excel and Power BI to evaluate revenue performance, profitability, product contribution, customer performance, and regional trends using the Superstore dataset.

## Tools & Technologies
- **Microsoft Excel:** Data cleaning, data validation, calculated fields, PivotTables, aggregation, and exploratory data analysis.
- **Power BI Desktop:** KPI development, data visualisation, comparative analysis, and dashboard reporting.

## Analytical Scope
- Sales and profit analysis by category, subcategory, and product.
- Customer-level sales aggregation and product performance ranking.
- Regional performance benchmarking.
- Monthly sales trend analysis.
- Identification of loss-making subcategories and profitability gaps.

## Key Findings
- Technology recorded the highest sales and profit among the three product categories.
- Furniture generated substantial revenue but comparatively low profit.
- The West region led in sales and profit, while Central showed relatively weak profit contribution.
- Tables, Bookcases, and Supplies recorded negative profit.

## Key Performance Indicators

| Metric | Value |
|---|---:|
| Total Sales | $2,297,200.86 |
| Total Profit | $286,397.02 |
| Profit Margin | 12.47% |
| Total Units Sold | 37,873 |

## Business Recommendations
- Investigate product-level drivers of negative profitability.
- Evaluate discounting, product mix, and cost structures within Furniture.
- Benchmark regional profitability to identify performance gaps.
- Leverage product and customer rankings to support sales planning and customer-retention strategies.
